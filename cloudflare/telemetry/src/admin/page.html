Current unreleased source defaults statistics on, preserves explicit off choices, and offers a one-time notice explaining the turn-off switch in Settings → Privacy & Security. Acknowledgment or dismissal is not consent and leaves statistics enabled; recording starts at startup and sending can begin about 30 seconds later, before acknowledgment. Without an endpoint, no recording, sending, or notice occurs. An installation is active on a local date once its report has arrived. Launch frequency and feature use are not counted. Released v0.1.0 defaulted on and released v0.2.0 defaulted off; history is not all opt-in. Dates are each installation's local dates, not a single global 24-hour window; today is the viewer's local date. Reports can arrive late or after offline use, so past dates can change and today's counts are incomplete. Unreported installations are absent; this is not a census of all installs. First seen is an ID's earliest reported activity date, not its actual install date. Cumulative observed installations count distinct reported random IDs. Multiple devices, reinstalling with a new ID, or disabling and re-enabling statistics can add IDs, so these numbers are not real people, downloads, or total actual installs. Turning statistics off deletes local IDs and pending dates, not server history or requests already sent. History is retained. Reports are unauthenticated and can be forged; rate limiting does not verify installations.
