Active means an install sent statistics that day. Users who do not opt in and builds without an endpoint are excluded. Dates are the user's local dates; today uses the viewer's local date. An install is one random installation ID. Reinstalling or disabling and re-enabling statistics creates a new install. Reports are unauthenticated and can be forged; rate limiting does not verify installs. Today's totals continue to grow.
